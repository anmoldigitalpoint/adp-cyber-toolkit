Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

ANMOL DIGITAL POINT ("we", "our", "ADP Digital Suite") respects your privacy. This page explains, in plain language, how this website handles your data.

1. Your files stay on your device

Every tool on this website — PDF tools, image tools, the document scanner, the ID card printer, the passport photo maker and the resume builder — runs entirely in your own browser. Your photos, documents and PDFs are processed locally on your device and are never uploaded to our servers.

2. Information we do not collect

We do not require you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, documents, photographs, or the content of any file you process using our tools.

3. Local storage

The website uses your browser's local storage only to remember small preferences on your own device, such as your dark/light mode choice, your recently used tools list, and whether you've already seen the WhatsApp channel popup. This information never leaves your device.

4. Third-party libraries

Some tools load open-source JavaScript libraries from public content delivery networks (CDNs) to work in your browser. These libraries process your files locally and do not transmit them elsewhere as part of normal operation of this website.

5. WhatsApp Channel

If you choose to join our WhatsApp Channel, your interaction is governed by WhatsApp's own privacy policy, since that happens on WhatsApp's platform, not ours.

6. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you can reach ANMOL DIGITAL POINT at 6207120625, or via our WhatsApp Channel / Instagram / YouTube linked in the footer.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this page from time to time as the website's tools evolve. Please check back occasionally for the latest version.
